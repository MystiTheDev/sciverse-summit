Help Center
Joining a session
Enter the 6-character join code from your chair on the home page, then pick your delegate name.
Raising a motion
Open Motions, choose a type, optionally add a topic, and hit Raise Motion. The chair sees it live.
Voting
When the chair opens a ballot, go to Voting and cast For, Against, or Abstain before it closes.
Presenting timers
Use Present on Display on the Candor and Speakers pages to project a fullscreen stage.
Live segments
When the chair starts Candor, a banner with a live countdown appears on every page.
